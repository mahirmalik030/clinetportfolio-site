01

Industrial Precision & Zero-Downtime Thinking

Before machine learning pipelines and cyber attack vectors, I managed 20+ operators on high-speed laminators at Tetra Pak, and supervised continuous production quality at Prolife Foods in Australia. Real failure is not an error code — it's a halted line and a disrupted supply chain. This grounded me in operational telemetry, human-centered safety, and the principle that reliable systems are designed from the operational floor up.

2017 – 2020 · Tetra Pak & Prolife Foods
02

Tactical Cyber Defense & Adversarial Thinking

Secured A$672,000+ in defense-funded research with Defence Trailblazer, DEWC Services, DSTG, and Cisco. From simulating multi-stage attacks on military C2 networks to building an AI-driven Adaptive Dynamic Application Orchestrator that deploys tactical software in contested environments — I have spent years thinking like an adversary to build systems that survive one.

2020 – Present · DSTG, Cisco & DEWC Services
03

Human-Centric AI & Social Venture Impact

At Kindship, built a machine-learning recommendation engine that matches differently abled children with the right therapists — turning months of searching into minutes. At Migrova (Stone & Chalk / Antler), secured a A$100,000 Seed-Start grant to deploy AI-powered matching for international migrants. AI that doesn't reduce real human friction isn't doing its job.

2022 – 2024 · Kindship & Migrova
04

Academic Leadership, Research & Pedagogy

Lecturing at Adelaide University in Cybersecurity, AI, and Software Engineering. Published 12+ papers across flagship IEEE/ACM conferences and journals, led 15+ R&D projects, and served as elected HDR Representative and GEDI (Equity & Inclusion) Representative during my PhD — advocating for researcher wellbeing and inclusive academic environments.

2021 – Present · Adelaide University
