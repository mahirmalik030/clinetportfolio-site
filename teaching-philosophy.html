Teaching Philosophy

Principles that guide my teaching practice, curriculum design, and student engagement.

  • Industry Relevance
    I closely monitor evolving industry needs and align my teaching to ensure that students acquire knowledge and skills directly applicable to real-world professional environments.
  • Feedback-Driven Teaching
    I actively seek and value student feedback and continuously adapt teaching strategies based on student engagement and learning responses.
  • Organised and Well-Defined Structure
    I emphasise clear organisation across lectures, assessments, and evaluation rubrics, enabling students to plan effectively and follow the course with confidence from the outset.
  • Up-to-Date Content
    Given the rapidly evolving nature of computer science, I ensure course material reflects current technologies, practices, and research trends rather than outdated concepts.
