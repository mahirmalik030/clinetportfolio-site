Research Overview

My research lies at the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity, with two complementary research directions:

  1. AI for Cybersecurity
  2. Cybersecurity for AI

AI for Cybersecurity: I develop intelligent and adaptive cyber defence approaches using machine learning, reinforcement learning, LLMs, context engineering, and agentic AI. My research focuses AI-driven cyber risk detection, assessment, prioritization, and mitigation. I also investigate explainable and responsible AI to support transparent, accountable, and trustworthy security decision-making.

Cybersecurity for AI: I focus on making AI systems secure, resilient, privacy-preserving, and trustworthy throughout their lifecycle. This includes the end-to-end security of AI pipelines, from data collection and model development to deployment and operation, as well as the security of LLM and agentic AI systems against emerging attacks and vulnerabilities.
