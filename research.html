Research Overview

My research focuses on Cyber Security, Software Engineering, and Generative AI, with special emphasis on AI-driven, self-adaptive, and cyber-resilient architectures for critical infrastructure, cloud-native deployments, and defence systems.

Key research focus areas span self-adaptive security risk management frameworks and automated LLM-assisted vulnerability assessment, Large Concept Models (LCMs) with uncertainty-gated multi-agent reasoning, resilient resource-efficient microservice auto-scaling (Smart HPA), and tactical application orchestration in contested environments in collaboration with DSTG, CSIRO’s Data61, Defence Trailblazer, and global industry partners.

Research & Development Projects

Applied R&D initiatives bridging high-impact computer science theory with industrial and national security deployments.

The Future of AI: Exploring the Potential of Large Concept Models

Dec 2024 – Present

This project investigates the limitations of traditional Large Language Models and introduces Large Concept Models (LCMs) as an emerging paradigm for advanced AI reasoning. It highlights abstraction challenges and demonstrates how concept-level representations improve semantic reasoning and contextual understanding across complex domains.

Partner: CSIRO’s Data61 View Paper (arXiv)

ChatNVD: Advancing Cybersecurity Vulnerability Assessment with LLMs

Feb 2024 – Nov 2024

ChatNVD explores the application of Large Language Models to improve vulnerability assessment and interpretation by integrating the National Vulnerability Database (NVD) and delivering context-rich cybersecurity insights for DevSecOps workflows.

Partner: CSIRO’s Data61 & IEEE Access View Paper (arXiv)

Smart HPA: A Resource-Efficient Horizontal Pod Auto-scaler

Jun 2023 – Dec 2023

Smart HPA introduces a resource-efficient auto-scaling framework for microservice architectures operating in resource-constrained cloud environments, enabling coordinated scaling, reduced operational overhead, and improved system resilience.

Partners: Monash University, University of Melbourne

MicroControllers: Securing Microservices with Self-Adaptive Architecture

Sep 2023 – Nov 2023

This project proposes a microservice-based MAPE-K architecture to overcome the limitations of monolithic self-adaptive cybersecurity frameworks while supporting scalable, autonomous, and distributed security management.

Partners: Cyber Security CRC, Cisco, DSTG
